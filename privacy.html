NEXUS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

Nexus collects system usage logs, user details (Name, Register/Staff ID, Email), and academic project submissions required for institutional project management.

2. Use of Information

The collected data is exclusively used for academic evaluation, advisor allocation, group project tracking, and institutional authentication.

3. Data Security

All credentials and session interactions are protected with encrypted transmission and stored under institutional database access controls.

4. Contact Administration

For data removal or privacy concerns, contact admin@nexus.edu.
