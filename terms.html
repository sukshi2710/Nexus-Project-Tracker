NEXUS Terms & Corporate Governance

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Acceptable Use

Authorized access to the Nexus Academic Platform is restricted to registered students, faculty, and administrators. Unauthorized access attempts are monitored and logged.

2. Intellectual Property & Corporate Compliance

All project code, deliverables, and documentation uploaded to the platform remain the intellectual property of the institute or affiliated sponsoring corporate entities where applicable.

3. Account Integrity

Users are responsible for keeping login credentials confidential. Sharing accounts or credential spoofing violates system policies.
